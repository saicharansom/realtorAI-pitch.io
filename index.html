The problem

"I lose deals every week just because I couldn't respond fast enough."

The solution

Two AI-powered workflows, one platform.

🎬

Video Studio AI

Upload listing photos → AI writes a script → Seedance renders a cinematic 9:16 reel → captions & voiceover burned in. Ready in minutes, not hours.

🤖

Lead Agent 24/7

Autonomously qualifies buyers, renters & sellers over iMessage or web chat. Books showings, sends listing videos, fires reminders. Hands off to the human at the right moment.

Video Studio — how it works

Step 1
Upload photos
Drag & drop up to 20 listing images. Compressed client-side automatically.
→
Step 2
AI script
GLM-5 vision model reads the photos and writes a punchy hook, 3 beats & CTA.
→
Step 3
Seedance video
ByteDance ARK renders a cinematic 15-second 9:16 reel from the images.
→
Step 4
MP4 output
ffmpeg burns captions & AI voiceover. Download and post directly to Reels/TikTok.

Fallback chain: Cumulus native video → ffmpeg photo slideshow

Tech stack

AI & Video

  • GLM-5 via IonRouter — script gen, photo vision, lead qualification, TTS
  • Seedance (ByteDance ARK) — image-to-video generation, 9:16 cinematic reels
  • ffmpeg — text overlay, voiceover mixing, slideshow fallback

Platform

  • Butterbase — Postgres DB, file storage, serverless hosting
  • Photon Spectrum — iMessage send & receive SDK
  • React + Vite + Tailwind — frontend
  • Node.js + Express — backend API
